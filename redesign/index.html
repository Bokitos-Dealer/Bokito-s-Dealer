    All FAQs
    What is the minimum order quantity?

    It depends on the product and is shown on every product page, from 30 units for 1 L bottles up to 3,000 for the smallest dosing cups. You order in full cases, and the unit price drops at every volume tier.

    How fast do you ship?

    Orders leave our Netherlands warehouse the same or next business day. Orders placed before mid-afternoon usually go out the same day. Every shipment is tracked and insured.

    Do I pay import duty or customs fees?

    No. Our stock is already in the EU: import duty and import VAT are paid and customs is closed. You get a Dutch VAT invoice.

    Can you provide compliance documentation?

    Yes. Because we work directly with the manufacturer, we can supply the Declaration of Conformity, EN ISO 8317 certification and food-contact statements for the SKUs you order.

    Do you ship outside the EU?

    Yes. For the UK we handle the export paperwork. For other countries, send us the destination and we'll quote shipping and lead time.

    Is your stock genuine Chubby Gorilla?

    Always. Every unit is sourced directly from the manufacturer, with no counterfeits and no grey-market stock.

    Do you sell to consumers?

    No, we sell to businesses only. All prices exclude VAT.
