    FAQ

    Good questions

    What's the minimum order?

    One case of any product. Case sizes are shown on each bottle, and quantities always round up to whole cases.

    How do the volume tiers work?

    Your tier is set by the total quantity of a product in your quote, across all colour combinations. Order 300 natural bottles with red caps and 300 black bottles with blue caps, and both lines are priced at the 600-unit tier.

    Are these real-time prices?

    The prices you see are our current list prices. Your quote is confirmed by email, together with colour availability, shipping and delivery timing.

    Can I get bottles with my own branding?

    Tick the custom labels box when you request your quote, tell us what you have in mind in the notes, and we'll come back with options.

    Is my information stored anywhere?

    Your selection is kept only in this browser. Nothing is sent until you choose to email your quote.
